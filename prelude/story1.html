Prelude

Evening, the 7th of October, 2006.
It was the birthday - the birthday of a despot.
The one who has ruled my country longer than I have lived on this earth.
The world was blind back then; it chose not to notice the crimes.
The country was steadily turning into kleptocratic autocracy.
The news were a shadow of their former self.

On that day the despot has taken a life of a person,
who through her work, has showed who that despot really was.
