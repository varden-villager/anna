“I love my Homeland, my village Urus-Martan, because it is the most beautiful village in the world. Now I miss it very much. At night I dream that I am running to my school with my friends, my school bag in my hand.
Here in Ingushetia, planes and helicopters often fly over, and I get scared, as if I were back home. In the last war Russian soldiers killed my father. Mom searched for him everywhere. At last she found his body, dead, buried, mutilated. I was six then, my brother was 8, and my little sister was 11 months old. After everything that happened, I thought the war had ended forever. But after a short time it all began again.
Now every day you hear adults crying as they talk about relatives who were killed. I would like to live under a peaceful sky! But will it be?”
