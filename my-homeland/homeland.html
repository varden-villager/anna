My Homeland

These are the excerpts from Anna Politkovskaya’s article
Война никого не видит (“War sees no one”)
Written on 23 March 2000
Ingushetia, near the border with Chechnya
Outskirts of the village Yandare
A refugee camp called “Goskhoz”
(abbreviation of “state agricultural enterprise”)
A tent school, third grade, children aged 8 and 9
They were assigned to write an essay called “My Homeland”
