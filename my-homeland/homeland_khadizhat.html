“When we were given 48 hours, we left Grozny for Zakan-Yurt. A month passed, and we came back home. But on 22 September they started bombing our city again, and on 29 September we left for Ingushetia. I want to go home so much! Even though only ruins are left there. In Ingushetia it is calm, no bombing, but every morning I wake up thinking about my home. It was bombed, and then burned. Even if only ashes are left there, they are mine, my own.”

Khadizhat Dzhamaldinova
