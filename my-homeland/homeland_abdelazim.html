“I have only one homeland. It is the city of Grozny. It was the most beautiful city on the whole planet. But my beautiful city was destroyed by Russia, and together with the city, all of Chechnya and the people who live in it. Those whom Russia has not yet managed to destroy left for Ingushetia, and so did I. But I miss home. I want to go home so badly, even though I already know my home was bombed. But I still want to... Why do I want to live in my own home? So that I would have the right to do whatever I want, and no one would reproach me.
RUSSIA, LEAVE US ALONE, WE ARE SICK OF YOU. There are so few Chechens left as it is. GO HOME and put things in order in your own place, not ours...”

Abdelazim Makhauri
