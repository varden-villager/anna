“I always dreamed of seeing my homeland Chechnya free of terrorism. But here I am, studying in a cold tent, while Russian children study on computers in warm schools... I don’t know if Putin has a heart. But if he had a heart, he would not have started such a war. Putin thinks a human life is worth fifty kopecks. He is deeply mistaken. And he steals these lives from people. I would like Putin to know: we are people too. Before the war came to us, Chechnya was more beautiful than Moscow. I would like to go home and live there for the rest of my life.”

Ali Makaev
