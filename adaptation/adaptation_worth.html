Human life often means nothing.
That’s what this decade taught me,
With specific examples.
You are defined by your status, wealth
And whatever country you are from.
You can be a murderous head of state
And get handshakes, talks, meetings.
You can be a person who is hurt
By the ongoing injustices,
And you’ll feel the weight of the world
Crushing you.
The evil gets its praise.
The good slowly dies.
