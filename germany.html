The despot came to Germany.
A protester outside was screaming, “Mörder, Mörder!”
The despot just grinned at him.
He stood beside the country’s chancellor
And said:
“Her murder causes more damage to russia and the government than her publications ever did.”

It was a typical excuse — one I would come to hear many times from the regime as I grew older.

Two weeks later, another critic of the despot was murdered.
