And what did Russia gain from these two wars?
Is Chechnya at peace?
Isn’t it a region known for its ruthless ruler — The despot’s puppet?
Isn’t it known for disappearances and murder
Of those who dare to disagree?
Isn’t that the region
That masks its poverty and lack of human rights
With lavish skyscrapers and expensive cars?
Was it worth a decade of fighting?
Was it worth the imperial conquest of a territory —
While the people who live there
Remain hurt and unhappy?
